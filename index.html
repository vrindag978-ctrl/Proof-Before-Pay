Five quick tools to help you pause and verify before taking action.

Prototype note: these checks use local warning patterns. They do not contact banks, open websites, or guarantee an offer is safe. Image text must be entered manually in this version; OCR is not connected.

Payment Verification Assistant

Compare a claimed payment with order details. This prototype cannot connect to a bank or UPI provider, so it never marks a payment as independently confirmed.

Proof Authenticity Chain

A checklist of what is claimed, compared, and independently confirmed.

① Screenshot / claimed details submitted
② Amount compared with order
③ Payment checked independently
④ Decision recorded with evidence
A screenshot is not proof that money reached your account. Check your own official bank or payment app before dispatching goods.

AI Scam Message Detector

What this scanner can and cannot do

✓ Available nowImage upload and local preview, plus analysis of text you enter.
◷ Not included in this offline buildAutomatic OCR, forensic pixel-level manipulation detection, and payment-provider confirmation.
The system will not call an image genuine or fake based only on its appearance. Independently verify the transaction or offer.

Suspicious Link Checker

Inspect a URL for common risk indicators. This local checker does not visit the website or query a live threat database.

Safer link habits

✓ Check the exact domain spelling.
✓ Open the official website yourself instead of following an unexpected link.
✓ Never enter a UPI PIN or OTP to receive money.
✓ HTTPS alone does not prove a site is trustworthy.

Buyer & Seller Trust Checker

Review details supplied by the user and flag missing or inconsistent information. This is not an identity verification or background-check service.

Trust indicator ≠ identity proof

The result reflects the checklist you complete. It does not verify a person's real identity, criminal history, or honesty. Avoid uploading sensitive identity documents.

Official Source Verification

Create a structured verification checklist for a claim and record the source you consulted. This offline prototype does not search the live web automatically.

Source verification principles

✓ Navigate to the official domain independently.
✓ Check date, eligibility and exact wording.
✓ Look for corroboration from the responsible organisation.
✓ Treat absence of evidence as “unverified”, not automatically “fake”.

Responsible reporting

Avoid posting private data, accusations, personal addresses, or full account numbers. Use public business details only. Repeated reports can help spot patterns, but they do not prove wrongdoing.

Safe Apply Mode

Before applying for an internship, job, scholarship or training programme, complete the checks you can verify.

Remember

Legitimate employers generally do not require candidates to pay to guarantee selection. Some genuine training programmes have fees, so review the organisation and terms carefully rather than relying on one signal.
